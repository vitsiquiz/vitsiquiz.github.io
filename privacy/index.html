Privacy Policy — English summary

Effective: 2026-10-26 · Version 1. The Georgian text above is the policy; this is a short summary of it.

Who. Vitsi (ვიცი!) is a Georgian trivia game for iPhone, made and published by Nino Tsitsriashvili, an individual developer in Georgia. Contact: vitsiquiz@gmail.com.

No accounts. There is no sign-up, password, email or phone number. The app never sends your name, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or device name. There are no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting.

On your phone. Your progress (stages, stars, scores), game history and last-room data (for rejoining), settings and downloaded questions are stored only on your iPhone. They are part of your normal iPhone backup and are deleted with the app. We cannot see or restore them.

What the server sees, and for how long.

  • *Playing with friends (rooms):* the room code, your nickname (chosen from a list), a random one-time room token stored only as a hash, the room's questions, your answers (choice, time, points) and your last-seen time. A room, with everything in it including the copies of its live (Realtime) messages, is deleted within 1 hour of its last activity (usually in 50–60 minutes, always before 24 hours) and never kept longer than 24 hours.
  • *Question updates:* the server keeps one number per day — how many packs were downloaded that day — for 30 days. To limit downloads it also records an IP hash, exactly as for rooms (below).
  • *Status check:* the app asks whether rooms are on and which app version is required. It sends nothing.
  • *IP addresses:* every internet connection shows the server your IP address; ours is no exception. We use it only to limit abuse (too many rooms, code guessing or too many downloads). The server stores a one-way keyed hash of it, not the address, deleted within 1 hour; the key changes daily. Technical connection details (for example the iOS version) appear in Supabase's logs.

Legal basis. Room data is needed to run the game (performance of a contract); the IP hash prevents abuse (legitimate interest).

Supabase. Our server runs on Supabase, Inc. (processor), in the EU, Frankfurt, Germany. Supabase processes data on our instructions, within its own privacy policy and sub-processors (for example Cloudflare). Like any host, it keeps its own technical connection logs (including IP addresses) for its own period: 1 day. If Supabase ever stops letting us delete a room's live-message copies ourselves, Supabase removes them within 3–4 days. Supabase's policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

Sharing. We never sell, rent or share data with anyone. The only parties that technically see data are Supabase and its sub-processors.

A "report a question" feature will be added in a future version; it will send only the question ID, a reason from a list and the time.

Children. Vitsi is for players aged 13 and over. It is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights. Under the Georgian Law on Personal Data Protection and, in the EU, the GDPR, you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, and you may complain to a supervisory authority (Georgia: Personal Data Protection Service, https://pdps.ge; EU: your national authority). Data is stored in the EU (Germany) [check: adequacy status — Nino to verify]. In practice we cannot identify you: nothing on the server names you or your device, and room data and IP hashes are deleted within 1 hour, so an access request cannot be matched to a record and deletion has already happened. Progress on your phone is deleted when you delete the app. You can still write to vitsiquiz@gmail.com; we answer within the legal deadline: 10 working days (Georgian law) or 1 month (GDPR).

This website. Hosted on GitHub Pages. No cookies, analytics or forms. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Changes. A new version is posted on this page with its date. Significant changes are also noted in an app update.
