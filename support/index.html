Support — English

What is Vitsi? Vitsi (ვიცი!) is a Georgian-language trivia game for iPhone: history, sport, pop culture, geography, science and nature. Free, no ads, no account. Play alone through stages, or with friends by room code.

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, password or email.

Where is my progress stored? Only on your phone. It survives app updates. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and we cannot restore it — there is no copy on a server. Restoring your iPhone from a backup restores it too.

Do I need internet? Not for solo play; yes for practice with Max and for rooms.

How do I play with friends? One player creates a room and gets a 6-digit code. The others (2–4 players in total) enter the code, pick a nickname from the list, and the host starts: 10 questions, 15 seconds each.

Android? iPhone only.

iPad? The app is made for iPhone; on iPad it runs at iPhone size.

Rooms say "temporarily unavailable". Rooms depend on the server. Check your connection and try again in a few minutes. A lobby closes after 10 minutes and its code stops working — create a new room.

What data do you collect? The minimum, for a short time. Full details in the privacy policy.

Contact: vitsiquiz@gmail.com. We answer within the legal deadline: 10 working days (Georgian law) or 1 month (GDPR).
